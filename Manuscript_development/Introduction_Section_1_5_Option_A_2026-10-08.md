## 1.5 Research gap, novelty statement and what this research work conveys

The available experimental reports on laser powder bed fused AlSi10Mg combine failures, specimens that survive a specified stopping cycle, and changes in manufacturing and test conditions. Our audited literature cohort provides first-exposure stress and outcome records, but it does not provide a consistently joined, numeric pre-fatigue defect or surface measurement for every specimen. The focused question is therefore whether synthetic episodes generated from a fitted, stress-based probability model add predictive value when an **entire publication**, rather than individual specimens from the same campaign, is held out. Answering it requires treating runouts as right-censored observations and comparing augmentation with an experimental-only model that already accounts for source variation.

We assembled a versioned inventory of 199 original first exposures. The primary comparison uses 192 records at stress ratio (R=0.1): 166 failures and 26 runouts from Wu, Chen and Matušů, with each publication held out in turn. Pooled and source-marginal Weibull accelerated failure time baselines are compared with source-preserving synthetic self-distillation and a prespecified filter that retains synthetic episodes only from training sources with observed runout support. Synthetic episodes remain training interventions and are never counted as new experimental specimens. The main endpoint is censored negative log likelihood, averaged equally over the three held-out publications; failure and runout contributions are also examined separately.

The contribution is a traceable test of **when this particular stress-only augmentation procedure does not improve transfer**, alongside a diagnosis of the opposing failure and runout likelihood changes under source adjustment. The fixed runout-support filter is not a learned selection algorithm, and same-model synthetic episodes carry no independent physical measurements. The reported lack of consistent augmentation gain limits the claim to the evaluated publications and loading domain. Independently measured, specimen-linked pretest features could support a later feature-guided algorithm, but that remains a separate research question requiring a verified data join and untouched evaluation campaign.
