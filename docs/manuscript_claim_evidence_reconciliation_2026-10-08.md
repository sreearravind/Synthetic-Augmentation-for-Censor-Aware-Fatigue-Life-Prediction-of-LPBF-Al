# Manuscript evidence reconciliation and defensible claim

**Decision date:** 8 October 2026. This is an interpretation of the existing, frozen results; no new fit, reweighting, pool of test cohorts or post-score method selection was performed. The repository title and opening proposal remain a **research objective**, not a demonstrated selective-learning algorithm.

## What was actually evaluated

C0 is a pooled experimental-only Weibull AFT model; C1 is an experimental-only, source-marginal Weibull AFT with fixed study-effect SD τ=0.25. S0 uses source-preserving parametric pseudo-episodes from a C1 teacher. S1 takes the same draws but uses a **fixed eligibility filter** requiring a real training runout in the source. S1 has no learned gate. Generated records are training interventions, not new physical experiments. All reported test scores below are censored negative log likelihood (NLL) of log10 life, with a survival term for documented runouts; lower is better. Keep study counts, different stress ratios and failure/runout mixes visible.

These rows are **not one pooled score**. The primary statistic is an unweighted mean across three held-out publications. Beretta and Al-Zuhairi each have one publication-weighted sample, different event mixes and documented domain shifts; Romano is already in development. The five seeds reuse the same physical observations.

The [earlier Hamidi Nasab graph check](../results/m1_external_2026-10-06/report.md) compared M1 with E0 on a **different 66-row training fit** (not S0 or S1): 46 graph positions (40 failures/six runouts) gave M1−E0 NLL −0.0198, driven by runouts with worsened failure density. It was inspected during method development, has no second independent human glyph reading, and cannot independently corroborate S1. It is not numerically combinable with the v2 four-arm scores.

## Claim boundary

| Claim | Decision and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Literature-sourced, first-exposure, censor-aware pipeline with publication grouping, source provenance and proper likelihood scoring | **Supported as implemented** for v2 and the fixed external comparisons, within the stated extraction/overlap limits. |
| Source heterogeneity and a failure-versus-runout trade-off matter for stress-only prediction | **Supported as an observed pattern**: C1 improves runout scores versus C0 in primary v2 and Beretta but can worsen failure scores. The observed effect is not a universal statement about every LPBF platform. |
| Synthetic training reliably improves unseen-publication fatigue-life prediction | **Not supported**. The primary S1 decision rule failed; external aggregate differences are small and seed dependent, and Al-Zuhairi has no runouts. The Beretta result does not overturn the primary finding. |
| A novel learned utility gate or new ML algorithm has been implemented and validated | **Not supported**. S1 is a predetermined source filter, and S0/S1 generate from and refit the same M1 family. Self-distillation adds no new physical evidence. |
| Specimen-specific pretest defect/roughness prediction and across-condition/ratio generality | **Not supported**. Current C0/C1/S0/S1 use stress only. Beretta's 11 matched roughness IDs have unresolved timing/site selection near crack origins; Bartošák's eight pretest-XCT rows are LCF with no runouts and are outside HCF v2. The lone R=−1 sources cannot identify an independent ratio coefficient under the primary grouping. |
| Q1 journal suitability, statistically significant augmentation superiority, or an independent five-study confirmation | **No such determination follows** from this evidence. There are three primary publication folds and two differently scoped fixed-model external sources, one a preprint. Retests, reanalyses, subconditions and synthetic seeds do not increase the publication count. |

**Defensible present contribution, if writing now:** a transparent experimental-data audit and publication-held-out evaluation showing that **source-marginal censor-aware modelling is useful for runout likelihood, while same-teacher synthetic self-distillation and a fixed runout-support filter do not produce a consistent out-of-source gain**. Phrase the external Beretta and Al-Zuhairi differences as descriptive follow-up, not confirmation of a new algorithm. A title promising *selective synthetic augmentation for improved prediction* would exceed the evidence; the repository name remains a project hypothesis.

**Decision for the user's original new-algorithm objective:** it remains **open**. Do not retrofit S1 weights, thresholds or architecture after inspecting these scores and present Beretta or Al-Zuhairi again as untouched validation. The reserved [Strauß–Löwisch and Kempf campaigns](unscored_experimental_source_screen_2026-10-06.md) remain unscored. Their role as future tests should be retained for a genuinely specified subsequent method, subject to source overlap and graph-reading checks.

## Next single task

Run a **feature-readiness and method-feasibility gate**, not another score: list candidate HCF campaigns with individual pre-fatigue predictors joinable to first-exposure stress, life/event and an explicit runout bound; record measurement timing, site-selection rule, overlap and source count. Beretta's roughness and Bartošák's LCF data currently fail different parts of that gate. If a defensible pretest cohort is found, specify what **independent physical information** enters a new method and what training-only source splits could learn its utility before touching reserved tests. If not, decide openly whether to write the supported limits/negative-results paper, with a matching title and abstract, rather than claiming the initial algorithm objective was met.
