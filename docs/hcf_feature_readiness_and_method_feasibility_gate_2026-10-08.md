# HCF feature-readiness and method-feasibility gate — 8 October 2026

**Scope and decision.** This is a source/protocol audit, not a new model comparison. Among the publicly inspected LPBF AlSi10Mg high-cycle-fatigue (HCF) campaigns, **no additional publication has been verified to supply both a numeric predictor recorded for each specimen before its first fatigue exposure and a join to that specimen's stress, first-exposure failure/life or runout bound**. This is a statement about the inspected public files, not the entire literature or inaccessible raw data. The [frozen 199-row v2 inventory](../data/cohorts/development_v2_199_2026-10-06.csv), 192-row primary R=0.1 folds, prior external scores, and reserved test sources are unchanged. No source was admitted, model refitted, outcome scored, or synthetic data generated here.

## Admission rule and inspected campaigns

A prospectively usable predictor must have a documented measurement time before fatigue, a prespecified measurement region or rule that does not use the eventual crack origin, a numeric value and original specimen ID, and a defensible ID join to stress basis, R, loading protocol, first-exposure life/event or stop, and retest history. Publication independence and source overlap are checked separately. Treatment/group means may describe a source but cannot be copied into rows and represented as individually observed roughness or CT. A runout bound cannot be inferred from an S–N arrow alone when the test stop is unspecified.

## Method-feasibility consequence

The [four-arm fixed-fold experiment](development_v2_four_arm_results_2026-10-06.md) has **three independent R=0.1 publication holdouts**. S1 uses a predetermined eligibility rule based on whether a training source has an observed real runout; it is not a learned selection model. Its macro censored NLL is 1.27330 versus 1.27299 for the experimental-only C1 and does not meet the preregistered preliminary-positive rule. [Beretta and Al-Zuhairi follow-ups](manuscript_claim_evidence_reconciliation_2026-10-08.md) are descriptive, differently scoped, and already inspected/scored. They cannot be recycled as untouched evidence for a subsequently designed gate.

A learned source utility or specimen-selective synthetic gate would require a defined **independent signal** (for example, genuinely pretest defect or surface measurements, or a separately validated physics constraint), identifiable training-only outcomes at enough independent campaigns to estimate the gate and assess source transfer, and a fully fixed procedure before reserved evaluations. Three stress-only publication folds, a small fixed-filter contrast, and pseudo-records sampled from the same teacher do not establish that signal. This does **not** prove a new algorithm is impossible; it means the present feature-augmented novelty claim is **not presently implementable or testable from verified public HCF joins**. Keep the [Strauß–Löwisch and Kempf reserved sources](unscored_experimental_source_screen_2026-10-06.md) unscored and do not tune on their outcomes.

Recommend A as the manuscript that can be written from current evidence. Treat B as a future project unless the specific missing public join and independent physical signal are demonstrated. Do not run new scores or recast these already seen external sources as untouched tests to make B appear validated.
