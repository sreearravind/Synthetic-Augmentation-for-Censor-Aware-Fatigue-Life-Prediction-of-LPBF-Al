## Fixed M1 distribution and fit

Let `z = log10(N)` for observed failure cycles and `c = log10(C)` for a paper-supported right-censor bound. Let `x = log2(stress_amplitude_MPa / 100)`. All exact source stress fields are nominal amplitude; for the external graph, calculate amplitude as `0.45 × sigma_max_MPa` at R = 0.1. Use no outcome-derived or postfracture covariate. Do not fit a stress-ratio, surface-finish, build-orientation, or frequency coefficient: across these few studies these effects cannot be separated reliably from study and source protocol.

For study `s`, `b_s ~ Normal(0, tau²)`, `mu_is = alpha + beta*x_i + b_s`, with `beta <= 0`. Conditional on `b_s`, cycles `N` are Weibull with scale `10^mu_is` and common shape `k > 0`. For `h = ln(10)*k*(z-mu)`, `log f_Z(z) = log(ln(10)*k) + h - exp(h)`; for a runout `log S_Z(c) = -exp(ln(10)*k*(c-mu))`. There is no failure density at a runout's stop cycle.

**Primary shrinkage:** fix `tau = 0.25` log10 cycles before scoring. This represents a priori study-scale spread of roughly a factor `10^0.25 = 1.78`; it is a design assumption, not an estimated material constant. Fit `alpha, beta, log(k)` by maximum **marginal** observed-data likelihood. For each training study, integrate its product of conditional failure densities and runout survival terms over its single shared `b_s`. Sum the logs of those study integrals. Use deterministic 161-node Gauss–Hermite quadrature for the normal integration and log-sum-exp arithmetic. Use the E0 parameter box `alpha ∈ [0,12]`, `beta ∈ [-12,0]`, `log(k) ∈ [-4,3]`; start the optimizer at the E0 solution and at its two existing alternate starts, keep the best converged solution, and fail explicitly if none converges. These are numerical bounds, not fitted model-selection choices. Verify the marginal objective and predictions are stable under 321-node quadrature to 1e-5 log-likelihood units before using them; if not, increase quadrature nodes for every fit under a documented numerical-only revision, with no model/metric change.

For any held-out or external study, **integrate over a fresh** `b_new ~ Normal(0,tau²)`: `f_new(z|x) = E_b[f_Z(z|x,b)]` and `S_new(c|x) = E_b[S_Z(c|x,b)]`. Compute the two integrals independently with the same quadrature rule; never assign a training study intercept, fit a new intercept on the test outcomes, or infer `b_new` from other records in the test study. The external score is the sum of individual prospective marginal predictive contributions. It is not the joint likelihood of the external study after observing some of its labels.

**Sensitivity:** refit with `tau = 0.10` and `0.50` and report both, without selecting the best on the external source. If numerical bounds are hit, a fit fails, or the sign of the comparison depends on tau, disclose this. No validation-driven tau tuning or row-level random splits.

## Precision, scoring and reporting

The primary external calculation uses the agreed center readings of Hamidi Nasab's 46 positions. For each failure, the graph outcome is a read cycle count; for each runout, the censor bound is **fixed at 5 × 10^6 cycles** regardless of arrow position. The graph's maximum-stress ordinate is converted before prediction. Run all nine combinations of coordinated lower/center/upper failure-life and stress readings (3 × 3); perturb stress also for runouts, while their censor bound stays fixed. Use the recorded per-symbol reading intervals. Report the minimum and maximum score over these nine scenarios as a **reading envelope**, not a confidence interval, calibration model, or set of extra specimens. Do not make the external reading interval into a uniform probability kernel.

The primary score is mean right-censored negative log likelihood of `Z = log10(N)`, including failure density and runout survival. Report E0 and M1 on the **same 46 external positions**, overall and by VF/SB/MP, with event counts, failure-density sum, runout-survival sum, and their paired difference `M1 − E0` (negative favors M1). Secondary: failure-only absolute error of the predictive median in log10 cycles; define the mixture median by solving `S_new(10^z|x) = 0.5` and label this metric incomplete for censoring. Report stress amplitude outside the exact training range and limitations by finish. No specimen-level confidence interval, pooled study significance test, or claim of treatment causal effect is supported by one external campaign.

The 46 symbol positions were reconciled by vector and raster methods **operated by one analyst**. Reviewing the workbook does not demonstrate that a second human independently read each glyph. Until a separate reader checks the marker identities and three merged areas, call the external result *provisional graph-derived validation*, and do not say independently reader-validated. If adjudication changes primary membership, preserve this original lock and report a versioned protocol deviation; never silently substitute a more favorable subset.
