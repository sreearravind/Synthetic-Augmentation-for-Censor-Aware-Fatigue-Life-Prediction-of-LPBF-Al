# Targeted pretest-feature and runout source screen — 7 October 2026

**Scope and decision.** This is a follow-up to the [source gate](independent_pretest_feature_and_runout_source_gate_2026-10-07.md) and the [frozen v2 feature audit](development_v2_feature_and_grouped_fold_audit_2026-10-06.md). The targeted review of the user's Drive papers and two public Zenodo deposits found **no verified new independent AlSi10Mg cohort** with a numeric pretest roughness/XCT value linked to each original fatigue specimen *and* row-level stress, first-exposure life/event and runout bound. This bounded finding does not prove that no such source exists. **No v2 membership, folds, models, scores, or candidate rows were changed.**

## Screening standard

For feature-model admission, preserve the original specimen ID, proof the numeric feature was measured on that same specimen before its **first** fatigue exposure, stress basis and ratio, failure or right-censor stop, retest history, and independence from previously used campaigns. An average of measured specimens in a treatment group is group context, not the feature of every fatigue specimen. Postfracture initiator dimensions cannot become prospective predictors. A study without runouts may be a separate failure-only source, but cannot validate censoring.

## Narrow next check

Inspect the small Bartošák ZIP's two README files and fatigue-life CSV, then verify the eight defect files' IDs, measurement timing, loading, and event definitions against the preprint or final article. If they are *pretest* and same-ID, register a separately labelled LCF candidate with its own evaluation question; if defect features were selected from postfracture analysis or censor stops are absent, record that limitation and stop. For the existing HCF cohort, independently look for Wu's **public per-ID pretest CT summaries**; only a demonstrated join can justify a versioned feature-enriched cohort. Neither lead alters the frozen v2 comparison.
