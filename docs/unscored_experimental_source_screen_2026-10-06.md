## Source-role firewall and extraction contract

Kempf Figure 3 is now reserved for a **future test of a later synthetic method**, not a source for generator training, gate selection, preprocessing, stress-scale choice or architecture changes. No per-glyph cycles/stresses from that figure have been transcribed into the manuscript dataset in this task. The figure was viewed for eligibility, so it is **prospectively unscored, not visually blinded**. The result will represent **one** new experimental campaign across machine/treatment strata, with a substantial R = −1 and bending-domain shift from the primary exact axial training cohort. Figure 2's machine C as-built/T6 outcomes must not be counted again. A future extraction must retain each glyph, event, 10⁷ censor bound, uncertainty and exclusions, and document whether cited Kempf 2021 experiments are re-reports.

Fini remains a separate backup domain test. Candidate training studies require original failures to be extracted with source-specific stress basis and evidence tier. Arrow-only studies without an independently supported stop contribute **no right-censored rows**; retest studies require first-exposure identity. No source in this screen has been added to the 66 exact or 26 graph records, and no new specimen count is claimed.

**Next data task:** digitize candidate training failures and resolve the first-exposure links, prioritizing Muhammad and Fernandes (with their arrows excluded) and the Roveda/Matušů linkage audits. Keep the reserved Kempf campaign sealed for scoring until the synthetic generator, accept/reject rule, comparators and metrics are fixed.
