## Source-role firewall and extraction contract

Strauß and Löwisch Figure 3 is reserved as the **primary future test of a later synthetic method**, with Kempf Figure 3 as a secondary bending-domain test. Neither source is available for generator training, gate selection, preprocessing, stress-scale choice or architecture changes. No per-glyph cycles/stresses from either figure have been transcribed into the manuscript dataset in this task. Both were viewed for eligibility, so they are **prospectively unscored, not visually blinded**. Figure 3 of Strauß and Löwisch has two axial R strata, but is **one** campaign; its Fig. 4 rotating-bending results may be evaluated separately after a separate rule, not silently pooled into the primary score. Kempf Fig. 2 repeats machine C as-built/T6 conditions shown in its Fig. 3. A future extraction must retain each glyph, event, paper-supported 10⁷ censor bound, reading uncertainty and exclusions. Check cited earlier studies for re-reporting and count each source campaign once.

Fini remains a separate backup domain test. Candidate training studies require original failures to be extracted with source-specific stress basis and evidence tier. Arrow-only studies without an independently supported stop contribute **no right-censored rows**; retest studies require first-exposure identity. No source in this screen has been added to the 66 exact or 26 graph records, and no new specimen count is claimed.

**Remaining folder triage:** Romano's separate PDF and the Wu/Chen/Zhang 2022/Glodež/Hamidi Nasab files represent previously used campaigns. Titles devoted to cyclic deformation (10, 23), notch-only transfer (11), composite material (30), and crack growth/fracture toughness (33) do not directly supply new smooth AlSi10Mg first-exposure HCF life rows for the main cohort. Very-high-cycle/ultrasonic papers (3, 15, 20–22, 26–27, 32, 36) require a separate frequency/cycle-domain protocol and possible overlap audit; paper 32 was previously screened as Pan and Hong 2024. Machine-learning/defect-model papers (6, 25) require provenance checking rather than automatic counting of their cited or reused test records. These are title/protocol triage decisions, **not claims of complete per-figure extraction**.

**Next data task:** digitize candidate training failures and resolve first-exposure links, prioritizing Muhammad and Fernandes (with their arrows excluded) and the Roveda/Matušů linkage audits. Keep both reserved campaigns unscored until the synthetic generator, accept/reject rule, comparators and metrics are fixed.
