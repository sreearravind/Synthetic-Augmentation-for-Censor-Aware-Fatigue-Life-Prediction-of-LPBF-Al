# Additional graph-derived training failures: Muhammad 2023 and Fernandes 2024

**Extraction date:** 6 October 2026. **Second-modality check:** same date. This is a candidate training tier; no algorithm was fitted or evaluated in this step. It contains **53 included failure glyphs** (38 Muhammad, 15 Fernandes) and an audit of all **88 inspected vector glyph paths** (61 and 27, respectively). A glyph is a plotted observation, not a verified uniquely identified specimen. Read these counts as qualified graph observations, never as an SCI/Q1 sample-size requirement.

## Reading and adjudication

The scripts `scripts/inspect_muhammad_fernandes_vectors.py` and `scripts/build_additional_training_failures.py` require PyMuPDF and Pillow for the first step. Check both PDF hashes. The first script finds glyph drawing paths and creates annotated crops and raw candidate JSON; the second applies the recorded glyph decisions and calibrates the axes. It writes `additional_training_failures_2026-10-06.csv` and `additional_training_symbol_audit_2026-10-06.csv`. The PDF pages are numbered as a reader sees them (1 based). Paths were checked against the figure visually. A separate `scripts/check_graph_glyphs_raster.py` rendered the PDFs at 8× and measured series-colored pixels and raster centroid offsets for all 88 candidates, recorded in `independent_raster_glyph_check_2026-10-06.csv`. This is **an independent image modality reading of the same figure**, not independent people, original numerical data, or a second specimen census. Fitted lines can contribute colored pixels; the CSV preserves centers and drawing sequence for rechecking.

Among the 53 retained glyphs, the median absolute vector-to-raster color-centroid displacement was 0.066 PDF pt in x and 0.131 in y (Muhammad), and 0.157 and 0.199 pt (Fernandes); none exceeded 1 PDF pt on either axis. These are **diagnostic displacements**, not bounds on experimental error: triangle centroids and trend lines need not coincide exactly with glyph bounding-box centers. FE02 had an especially asymmetric blue pixel pattern (quadrants 242;74;200;34) and x displacement −1.389 pt. FE19 had only partial orange circumference alongside the red triangle. Direct inspection of the unmarked figure prompted holding both, beyond the six originally held overprints. This pass did not restore any previously held marks.

For Muhammad, log10(N) is linear from x=146.943 at 10⁴ to x=457.148 at 10⁷; Δσ=0 at y=289.144 and changes 10 MPa per 5.0552 PDF points. For Fernandes, log10(N) is linear from x=73.832 at 10⁴ to x=278.284 at 10⁷, while Δσ varies logarithmically with y: 200 MPa at y=525.6605 and 100 MPa at y=606.4665. Both plot **stress range**, so the candidate input stress amplitude is **Δσ/2**. The interval columns use the plotted glyph half-size plus 0.5 PDF point per axis as a transparent reading allowance; they are **not** measured specimen uncertainty or statistical confidence intervals. Rounded coordinates and amplitudes are approximations.

**Included:** only apparently original smooth/round-bar failure glyphs with no runout arrow or unresolved symbol overlap. The included rows all have `event=1`, `censoring=failure`, `source_precision_tier=graph_derived`, and `training_role=candidate_training_only`. The papers do not link plot glyphs to unique specimen IDs.

**Held:** Muhammad MU18, MU31 and MU50 sit on the 10⁷ right boundary without verified individual outcomes; close same-series clusters and two obscured black marks are held. Fernandes FE02 (blue square mostly covered by treatment glyphs) and FE19 (partly covered orange circle in that cluster) were further held after the raster review, alongside six already held cross-series overprints. Fernandes FE06, FE07, FE17 and FE27 have black runout arrows; the paper identifies arrows as runouts but a stopping cycle for these individual glyphs was not verified. FE26 is a separate left orange circle without an attached arrow. Exact reasons and centers are in the symbol audit. The Muhammad figure/path count conflict warrants author-data or independent figure review before using these rows as a specimen census; the sparse AB R=−1 subgroup must not support a subgroup claim.

## Research use boundary

Keep the 53 rows in a **separate graph-derived candidate tier**, with source/study grouping. They have not been merged into an exact-only core or used to tune a likelihood. Fit and assess any future model with source-aware weighting and whole-study separation; report all 53 as *graph observations pending original-data confirmation*, not as 53 verified independent specimens. No synthetic samples are included. No Strauß and Löwisch 2024 or Kempf 2022 marks were digitized, trained on, or scored here; both remain reserved prospective external tests. The Hamidi Nasab comparison has already been used and is not a new external confirmation.

**Reproduce:** supply the two source PDFs and run the vector script with `--muhammad`, `--fernandes`, and `--output candidate_dir`. Run the raster script with both PDFs, `--candidates candidate_dir` and `--out raster_csv`. Then run the build script with `--candidates candidate_dir --out output_dir`. Candidate JSON snapshots are kept beside the CSVs to make this particular reading reproducible. Visual adjudication is encoded in the build script and should be revisited if a clearer specimen table is obtained.
