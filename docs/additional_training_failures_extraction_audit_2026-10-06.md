A glyph is a plotted observation, not a verified uniquely identified specimen. Read these counts as qualified graph observations, never as an SCI/Q1 sample-size requirement.

## Reading and adjudication

The scripts `scripts/inspect_muhammad_fernandes_vectors.py` and `scripts/build_additional_training_failures.py` require PyMuPDF and Pillow for the first step. Check both PDF hashes. The first script finds glyph drawing paths and creates annotated crops and raw candidate JSON; the second applies the recorded glyph decisions and calibrates the axes. It writes `additional_training_failures_2026-10-06.csv` and `additional_training_symbol_audit_2026-10-06.csv`. The PDF pages are numbered as a reader sees them (1 based). Paths were checked against the figure visually, but this is **one vector reading with visual review**, not two independent human digitizations. The CSV preserves center coordinates and drawing sequence for rechecking.

For Muhammad, log10(N) is linear from x=146.943 at 10⁴ to x=457.148 at 10⁷; Δσ=0 at y=289.144 and changes 10 MPa per 5.0552 PDF points. For Fernandes, log10(N) is linear from x=73.832 at 10⁴ to x=278.284 at 10⁷, while Δσ varies logarithmically with y: 200 MPa at y=525.6605 and 100 MPa at y=606.4665. Both plot **stress range**, so the candidate input stress amplitude is **Δσ/2**. The interval columns use the plotted glyph half-size plus 0.5 PDF point per axis as a transparent reading allowance; they are **not** measured specimen uncertainty or statistical confidence intervals. Rounded coordinates and amplitudes are approximations.

**Included:** only first-exposure smooth/round-bar glyphs with no runout arrow or unresolved symbol overlap. The included rows all have `event=1`, `censoring=failure`, `source_precision_tier=graph_derived`, and `training_role=candidate_training_only`.

**Held:** Muhammad MU18, MU31 and MU50 sit on the 10⁷ right boundary without verified individual outcomes; close same-series clusters, two obscured black marks, and overprinted Fernandes cross-series marks are held. Fernandes FE06, FE07, FE17 and FE27 have black runout arrows; the paper identifies arrows as runouts but a stopping cycle for these individual glyphs was not verified. FE26 is a separate left orange circle without an attached arrow. Exact reasons and centers are in the symbol audit. The Muhammad figure/path count conflict warrants author-data or independent figure review before using these rows as a specimen census; the sparse AB R=−1 subgroup must not support a subgroup claim.

## Research use boundary

Keep the 55 rows in a **separate graph-derived candidate tier**, with source/study grouping. They have not been merged into an exact-only core or used to tune a likelihood. Fit and assess any future model with source-aware weighting and whole-study separation; report all 55 as *graph observations pending independent validation*, not as 55 verified independent specimens. No synthetic samples are included. No Strauß and Löwisch 2024 or Kempf 2022 marks were digitized, trained on, or scored here; both remain reserved prospective external tests. The Hamidi Nasab comparison has already been used and is not a new external confirmation.

**Reproduce:** supply the two source PDFs and run the vector script with `--muhammad`, `--fernandes`, and `--output candidate_dir`. Then run the build script with `--candidates candidate_dir --out output_dir`. Candidate JSON snapshots are kept beside the CSVs to make this particular reading reproducible. Human adjudication is encoded in the build script and should be revisited if a clearer specimen table is obtained.
