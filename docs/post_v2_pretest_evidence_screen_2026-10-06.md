## What a usable new feature must mean

A predictor must have been knowable **before the specimen's first fatigue exposure** and joinable to that specimen's stress, observed failure life or right-censor bound. A pore identified as the crack origin after fracture, a post-cycling CT measurement, an average of another coupon, and a value chosen because it best explains the observed failure are distinct from a prospective pretest feature. For XCT, a defensible candidate is a predeclared functional of the *whole pretest gauge scan* (for example maximum surface-connected defect size or a near-surface extreme), with voxel/detection threshold retained. Geometry, stress basis, loading mode, ratio, frequency and stopping policy are required to judge transfer, not automatically fitted covariates. Preserve exact tables and graph readings as different precision tiers.

Existing v2 has stress amplitude for all 199 records, but no harmonized, matched pretest pore or numeric roughness field. Wu's source says **all 41 HCF specimens were CT-scanned before testing**, at 9 µm voxel size; this establishes measurement timing but the presently released v2 rows do not contain a verified per-ID CT descriptor. Do not substitute its post-mortem initiator for that missing predictor. Chen's reported initiator SEM fields are likewise after fracture. Matušů has source-linked processing series but powder, printer, finish and heat treatment are coupled across platforms. [v2 feature audit](development_v2_feature_and_grouped_fold_audit_2026-10-06.md); [Wu 2021 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2021.106317).

## Data contract for the next audit

For each candidate specimen, record `campaign_id`, original `specimen_id`, exposure number, material, build/platform/batch, geometry and nominal/local stress basis, amplitude conversion and R, fatigue mode/frequency, failure event or documented stop, source and graph-reading precision. For every proposed feature record `measurement_time` (before/after fatigue), `measurement_unit` (same specimen versus sibling/group), raw source locator, XCT voxel/detection threshold or profilometry protocol, and the rule selecting the defect/roughness summary. Include nonfailures in feature availability checks. Reject a predictor picked through its postfracture crack origin. Preserve relationships to existing Wu, Romano/Tognan, Chen and Matušů campaigns and to the reserved holdouts.

**Gate before v3 or a novel ML claim:** demonstrate an auditable specimen-level join for at least one independent original campaign with pretest features and outcomes, and another source or predeclared transfer setting to test generalization. This is an evidence requirement, not a fixed journal row-count threshold. If Afroz's plotted triplets cannot be reliably reconciled or Mendeley's files lack pretest specimen IDs, leave the v2 model conclusion unchanged.

**Next single task:** inspect the Mendeley dataset's file schema/provenance without training or scoring, and independently pair a small Afroz Fig. 9/Fig. 11 pilot to see whether an individual pretest Ra/Rz value and the same specimen's stress/life/runout can be recovered. Decide the dataset's role before opening all outcome rows.
