# Lehner 2024 versus Al-Zuhairi 2026: publication overlap adjudication

Audit date: 2026-10-07. Primary source: user-supplied Lehner et al., *International Journal of Fatigue* 187 (2024), 108479, DOI [10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2024.108479](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2024.108479), printed pp. 3–6, 9–10; compared with [Al-Zuhairi et al. 2026, preprint v1](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202609.1879), §2, Tables 1–3, and [S1–S13 audit](al_zuhairi_s1_s13_join_and_overlap_audit_2026-10-07.md). The 2024 paper says “Data will be made available on request”; no such author-supplied raw table is included in the 11-page PDF.

The 2024 introduction/materials prose describes its own PBF build and the 2026 preprint says its 36 specimens were manufactured and tested “in this work.” Neither source explicitly links or disclaims individual specimen IDs across papers. Their shared authorship, machine, powder chemistry and settings motivate caution, not automatic deduplication. The 2024 graph cannot be made into exact source data by fitting a curve and inferring IDs.

## Candidate disposition, without waiting for authors

1. **23 rows, 30 µm (S1/S2/S5/S6):** no overlap with Lehner 2024's reported 60 µm specimens on manufacturing grounds. Keep as preprint-tier, source-separated, exact-outcome candidates. They may be predeclared for an R = −1 **outcome-only external challenge** after feature compatibility and evaluation-role checks. They have no explicit runout and cannot assess runout calibration or censor-aware gains. This is not a claim of independence from every other paper.
2. **12 rows, 60 µm (S3/S4):** unresolved possible reuse of Lehner 2024; exclude from any training, fold, pooled score or supposedly independent external test that could double-count the platform. They can remain in the candidate ledger for source audit. An author response is optional for present progress, but would be necessary for categorical first-exposure attribution if no other primary evidence emerges.
3. **Whole source:** the 199-row v2 cohort and its folds remain frozen. The 35-row transcription is not a specimen-matched confocal-feature dataset, because S7–S13 lack keys. Never use postfracture crack-initiator measurements as pretest inputs.

The [35-row candidate CSV](../data/candidates/al_zuhairi_2026_s1_s6_35_exact_outcome_candidate_2026-10-07.csv) now flags the 23/12 source-overlap tiers row by row. The missing sixth 30 µm V AB specimen is neither invented nor labelled a runout.
