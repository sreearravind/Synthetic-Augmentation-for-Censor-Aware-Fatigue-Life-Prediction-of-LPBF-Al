## Highest-priority lead: Beretta et al. (2022), ESA benchmark

Primary article: [Beretta et al., Materials & Design 218, 110713](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matdes.2022.110713); [institutional record and open article](https://research.utwente.nl/en/publications/a-benchmark-activity-on-the-fatigue-life-assessment-of-alsi10mg-c/). The author-posted article text is also [publicly readable](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/360440671_A_benchmark_activity_on_the_fatigue_life_assessment_of_AlSi10Mg_components_manufactured_by_L-PBF). It reports newly manufactured builds numbered 242–245, including **23 as-built and 17 machined cylindrical axial fatigue specimens** and 30 benchmark components. The cylindrical tests used nominal R=0.1, about 35 Hz, a 10% stiffness reduction as the failure definition and **5×10^6 cycles as the first-exposure runout stop**. Runouts were then retested at higher load; Table 3 indicates four additional AB tests and four M tests in parentheses, which are repeated exposures, not eight more independent specimens. Components have a different **10^7-cycle** stop and geometry; do not pool them as cylindrical rows. The paper says the campaign's data are in its Supplementary Material.

**Same-ID measured feature lead:** Appendix A Table 18 lists roughness (`Rt`, `Rv`, and inferred equivalent `sqrt(area)`) for **11 named AB cylindrical specimens**: FN1-245, FN2-245, FN3-245, FN4-245, FN5-245, FN7-245, FN3-243, FN4-243, FN5-243, FN6-243, FN7-243. This is a published specimen-ID feature table rather than group means. Table 19 separately lists component IDs and must stay in a component domain. Table 2's metallographic porosity is from HC carrier samples and cannot be assigned to individual fatigue specimens. The methods call the roughness instrument a Keyence confocal microscope while the appendix table header says “Stylus”; record this as an instrument-description discrepancy, not silently resolved.

**Critical gate still open:** Section 3.7 states that cylindrical roughness lines were taken *in the vicinity of crack initiation sites*, and component measurements selected the fracture side. The article does not establish in the inspected text that the Appendix A values were fixed before the fatigue test, or that those lines were selected independently of the observed initiation site. A physically existing as-built surface does not make an outcome-selected measurement a prospective predictor. It is possible that a prespecified four-area whole-gauge summary is usable, but that must be documented from the original method/raw record. Similarly, the public article shows an aggregate S–N plot; the exact per-ID stress, life, event and first-exposure/retest linkage must be checked in the cited supplement. No numeric feature/outcome join has been admitted yet.

**Independence:** These numbered 242–245 builds are presented as a new campaign. Check the original build/date and specimen crosswalk against the frozen Romano/ESA rows before calling any matching rows independent. Do not infer independence merely from publication year or a changed figure.
