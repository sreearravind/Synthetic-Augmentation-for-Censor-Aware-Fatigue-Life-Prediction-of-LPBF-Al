## Workbook reconciliation

The AlSi10Mg fatigue workbook in the new folder is byte-for-byte identical to the copy already audited (SHA-256 `ddfd60e4ddefc7f499399403e5f958d61b792d8cbe70f22933ec87ca84e4a357`). It has 72 populated records in A:C and 72 in H:J; **the complete multisets of (literal ID, maximum stress, evaluated 2Nf) are identical**, including multiplicity. A:C computes all 72 life cells with formulas; H:J gives numeric counterparts sorted/grouped by thickness/geometry. **Do not count 144 independent records.**

There are **70 distinct literal ID strings** among 72 entries. `T1L_2 (19)` appears twice at the same maximum stress with *different* recorded lives, as does `T3S_5 (4)`. These may represent a labeling error or multiple exposures; they are **not resolved by duplicate removal or by averaging**. `T1S`, `T1L`, and `T3w` are incomplete-looking IDs with numeric outcomes. Six left-block formulas sum two numeric terms before multiplying by two (left rows 7, 10, 33, 34, 62, 75). The workbook does not say whether those terms are interrupted intervals, load steps or something else; it gives neither first-exposure event flags nor a censoring field. `2Nf` is headed “Reversals to Failure”, but its exact meaning, runout treatment and any conversion to cycles must be established from the experimental protocol.

The [ID-only ledger](../data/candidates/rahmdel_id_only_structure_2026-10-07.csv) preserves each of the 72 records' left/right row locators, raw ID, nominal thickness (1, 2, 3 mm), geometry (wall, small block, large block), duplicate-ID flag, six formula-segmentation flags and candidate XCT file match. It intentionally excludes stresses and fatigue lives. It has four plausible XLSX-to-fatigue ID matches, five XCT XLSX filenames without a corresponding literal/format-normalized fatigue ID, and **zero verified prospective pretest-feature joins**.

## Protocol and overlap status

The deposit [description](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/w4vsnk9z6w/1) names raw fatigue tests, XCT data and fracture images for AlSi10Mg and 17-4 PH with three sizes and three geometries. It does **not** establish scan timing relative to first loading, XCT voxel/region/segmentation rules, cyclic frequency, stress ratio, event/runout encoding, or a publication/specimen map. The fracture images are outcome-conditioned and not prospective predictors.

A search of relevant primary publications identified [Tahmasbi et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2024.108544) (five geometry families, 20 kHz, R=−1) and [Poudel et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engfailanal.2025.109602) (cylindrical notched AlSi10Mg/17-4 PH). Their shared alloy/institution or use of blocks is **not** proof that this wall/small-block/large-block T1/T2/T3 workbook is the same campaign or a new one. There is no verified overlap resolution.

## TIFF decision and next evidence request

**Do not upload TIFF files now.** Statistical XLSX files are sufficient for the schema and candidate-ID audit. A TIFF, including one from a directly matching folder such as `F_T2L_2 (1)` or `F_T3W_8 (1)`, could later help verify the ROI/defect extraction, but image pixels alone do not establish that the scan was acquired **before** the fatigue test, nor do they resolve runouts or duplicate IDs. A selected TIFF pilot becomes useful only after the timing and record semantics are documented.

The most valuable next evidence is an original README, lab protocol, companion experimental article, or specimen-level manifest that states: (1) test mode, R, frequency, stress basis and first exposure; (2) whether the tabulated `2Nf` is reversals or cycles and the stopping/event rule; (3) identities/explanations for the two repeated IDs and six two-term formulas; (4) XCT date/timing relative to each specimen's first fatigue test, voxel size, ROI and erosion threshold; (5) relationship to the Tahmasbi/Poudel campaigns. A dated pretest XCT acquisition log with corresponding fatigue test dates would also resolve timing. No author correspondence is assumed necessary; continue a no-requisition source search if such records are public.

**Model boundary:** Rahmdel remains a quarantined candidate external campaign. No Mendeley rows join v2, no tuning or scoring is conducted, and the five Afroz plotted glyphs remain separate graph-approximate pilot evidence. The frozen v2 cohort remains 199 rows.
