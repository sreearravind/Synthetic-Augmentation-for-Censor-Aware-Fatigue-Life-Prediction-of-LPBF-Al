# Independent experimental source screen and external holdout lock

**Decision date:** 2 October 2026. **Status:** source identity and inclusion rules locked; specimen digitization and quality control pending. No model was fitted or scored on the sources below. Published figures were inspected for eligibility, so the outcomes are not literally unseen; the prospective constraint is that their numerical values and model scores will not guide subsequent model design.

## Existing development evidence

The exact development cohort comprises 66 smooth first-exposure records from Wu 2021, Romano 2018 (including its Tognan 2024 re-report), and Chen 2024. Zhang 2022 and Glodež 2020 supply the already-used graph tier. These five underlying experimental studies are **ineligible** for external confirmation. The previously examined Roveda 2024 Table 4 episodes have unresolved repeat-test linkage and are also ineligible until adjudicated. A manuscript citation to an existing study does not by itself make a new experimental campaign duplicate data; specimen and build provenance must be checked.

## Frozen primary test rule

1. **Source unit:** all identifiable original specimen glyphs in Hamidi Nasab Fig. 10, across the three finish conditions, with one row per first fatigue exposure. Do not take fitted dashed curves, literature comparison values in Table 5, or summary fatigue limits as specimens. Keep ambiguous overlapping glyphs in an exclusion log rather than inventing a count.
2. **Stress convention:** the figure's vertical coordinate is maximum stress, not amplitude. With R = 0.1, calculate nominal amplitude as (1 - R) × σmax / 2 = 0.45 σmax. Retain the original ordinate and the conversion in every extracted row. Never mix these values with the stress-range ordinate Δσ of Muhammad Fig. 10, for which σa = Δσ / 2.
3. **Events:** arrows in Hamidi Nasab Fig. 10 are right-censored lower bounds at the stated 5 × 10⁶ cycles. A failure symbol contributes a failure at its plotted cycles. Verify ambiguous glyph types independently; do not treat the arrowhead's horizontal position as a measured failure life.
4. **Reading precision:** two readers independently identify symbol, series, stress, and life for every included glyph. Preserve both readings, the agreed central reading, a plausible reading interval, and the reason for exclusion or adjudication. Digitization disagreement does not create extra specimens. Fixed source-table information takes priority over graph inference.
5. **Model firewall:** no Hamidi Nasab or Muhammad specimen is used for fitting, tuning, preprocessing, study offsets, graph-reading error calibration, or synthetic generation. Freeze model architecture, hyperparameter/search rule, graph handling, evaluation metrics, and the readable-glyph inclusion log before opening holdout scores. Do not use a held-out study's fitted intercept; integrate over a new-study effect if that model is adopted. Record scores once and report source/finish strata even when unfavorable.
6. **Scope of confirmation:** the primary result will be **independent graph-extracted experimental validation**, not an exact-table external validation. It is one new experimental campaign, with potentially heterogeneous finishes. Its point count does not substitute for independent study count or guarantee Q1 suitability. Any change made after inspecting its scores must be labelled exploratory and revalidated on another unused campaign.
