## Admission rule

A usable row must identify the original specimen, link a numeric feature measured before its first fatigue exposure, give the applied stress definition and ratio, and distinguish failure from a first-exposure runout with its stopping cycle. Group averages, postfracture critical defects, and retrospectively selected XCT pores cannot stand in for pretest per-specimen features. Duplicate publication of a campaign must be traced.

## Search limits and next decision

The targeted searches used exact article titles/DOIs and KH/LoF roughness terms against publisher pages, KIT/UCLouvain institutional records, and indexed Zenodo/Figshare/Mendeley results. A failed search is not evidence of absence from every repository. Kramer's availability statement explicitly leaves an author-request path, which is outside the present no-requisition approach. The separate van der Rest supplement is **known to exist** from its article, but could not be directly inspected here.

**Next single task:** obtain and audit van der Rest's actual publisher supplementary Tables S1–S3. Check whether they give each original first-exposure specimen's stress maximum/amplitude, life, failure/runout at the stated 10^7-cycle stop, process/finish, and its own *pretest* roughness or XCT statistic. If they give only failure-initiation categories, classify the campaign as outcome-only rather than assign a group-average predictor. Cross-check any overlapping thesis/publication before calling it independent. No model comparison should be run as part of that data audit.
