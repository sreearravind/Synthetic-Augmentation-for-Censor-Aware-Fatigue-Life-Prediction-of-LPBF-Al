## Afroz: sought corroboration of loading and roughness timing

The [figure and stress audit](afroz_figure_and_stress_basis_audit_2026-10-07.md) remains in force. The [Springer article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40964-024-00759-x) states axial tension–tension R=0.1 in §2.2, Table 4 and the legends, but Table 5 gives σa/σmax=0.55 rather than (1−0.1)/2=0.45. The secondary Fig. 9a axis does not resolve it. §2.5 says Ra/Rz are averages of five lines **on each fatigue specimen**, while Fig. 11 joins roughness and life; the exact time of profilometry relative to the first loading cycle is not stated. Targeted searches for an official correction, supplementary raw file, independent protocol or load history found no evidence that would choose between these conflicting R/amplitude definitions. An [earlier SSRN version](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4548909) was indexed but its text/PDF could not be checked in this screen, so it cannot be cited as corroboration.

**Disposition:** continue quarantining all five Afroz pilot glyphs. A useful author/protocol clarification would give the machine's σmax and σmin or programmed ratio, specify which Table 5/axis field is erroneous, and say whether Ra/Rz were measured after finishing and **before first fatigue loading**. No message to authors was sent.
