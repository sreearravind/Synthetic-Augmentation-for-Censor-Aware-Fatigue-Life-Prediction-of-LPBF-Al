# Independent pretest-feature and runout source gate — 7 October 2026

**Decision:** The targeted audit does not yet establish a new AlSi10Mg campaign with all of (a) independently identifiable first-exposure specimens, (b) a **numeric, same-specimen predictor measured before fatigue**, (c) unambiguous stress basis and ratio, and (d) row-level failure/runout bounds. This is a finding about sources screened, not a claim that no such dataset exists. The frozen [v2 199-row cohort](../data/cohorts/development_v2_199_2026-10-06.csv), [four-arm development result](development_v2_four_arm_results_2026-10-06.md), and [Al-Zuhairi external score](al_zuhairi_30um_external_outcome_only_results_2026-10-07.md) are unchanged. No outcomes were scored or used to select a new model in this audit.

## Eligibility checks

A feature must be measured on the **same specimen before its first fatigue exposure** and be joinable by a defensible original ID or unambiguous graph correspondence. A process/treatment-group mean is not a specimen-level feature. A defect chosen from the eventual fracture origin is not a pretest input, even if a CT volume exists. Every runout requires a recorded first-exposure stop/bound and a link to any later retest. Confirm stress *amplitude* versus maximum/range, R, loading mode/frequency, material and process domain. Check shared campaigns across papers before counting independent evidence.

## What can be done next

**Next single data task:** transcribe Kramer's **19 KH/LoF Appendix A2 rows** into a *candidate, outcome-only* ledger, checking all reported failures and the KH runout at 10⁷ cycles against the original table. Mark material, R=−1, 50 Hz, rotating-bending, process family, stress-amplitude basis, 10⁷ stop, absence of per-ID Sa/Sz, and the HD/Wexel reuse exclusion. This creates a defensible additional independent **censoring outcome** source without pretending it has a specimen-level feature. Keep it outside v2 and outside an already scored validation comparison until its role is predeclared. It does not by itself authorize an algorithm or novelty claim.

In parallel, a **public** Rahmdel methods/scan manifest or openly released per-ID XCT/roughness table for a second original campaign would be required to cross the feature gate. The Baig paper is a useful near-match but its requested-data route does not presently satisfy the no-requisition path. Do not use group-average roughness or postfracture initiator sizes to force a joined dataset. There is no fixed Q1 specimen-count rule; independently reproducible joins and a true held-out publication matter more than an arbitrary number.
