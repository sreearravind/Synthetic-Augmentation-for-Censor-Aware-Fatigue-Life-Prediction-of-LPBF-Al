## Fixed-model comparison, before any score

1. Reuse the **already archived** 192-real-row all-R=0.1 fit parameters in [`romano_transfer_fit_parameters.csv`](../results/development_v2/romano_transfer_fit_parameters.csv), blob `72bb25ca79d72effd2c2bbac4c30fffcbc80ed19`. It contains one C0 and one C1 fit and five S0/S1 fits each at seeds 13, 29, 47, 71, 101. Verify success, finite values, fixed `τ=0.25` for M1 and the exact blob before predictions. Do not fit, select, calibrate or infer a Beretta study offset from these outcomes. C1 is the experimental-only comparison anchor; C0, S0 and S1 are reported as fixed secondary arms. The synthetic arms previously failed the predeclared primary development advantage rule, so a favorable external score alone would not reverse that finding.
2. Read the 32-row masked input; require unique composite IDs, `exposure_index=1`, R=0.1, `σa=Δσ/2` and the above blobs. For every arm/seed, save keyed predictive parameters and distribution forecasts **before joining outcomes**, with the stress transform `x=log2(σa/100 MPa)`. C0 uses no source offset. C1/S0/S1 integrate a **fresh** `N(0,0.25²)` source offset for every predictive density or survival probability, never a posterior learned from Beretta. A missing seed or failed 161/321-node numerical agreement at `1e-5` invalidates that arm's fixed comparison; do not substitute another fit.
3. Join outcomes from the candidate ledger strictly by composite ID, surface, batch, original Δσ, derived amplitude and first exposure; require 25 failures and seven right-censored 5×10⁶ bounds. For an observed failure `z=log10(N)`, score `-ln f_Z(z|σa)`; for a runout score `-ln S_Z(log10(5×10⁶)|σa)`. The density is with respect to **log10 cycles**, and `ln` is natural logarithm. Use the same censor operator for every arm. Never score a retest failure as an independent specimen.
4. Report the single-study 32-row mean censored NLL for C0/C1 and each of the five S0/S1 seeds, their fixed-seed mean and min–max, and paired per-ID differences from C1. Show separate failure-density (25) and runout-survival (7) mean NLLs; report as-built/machined counts and descriptive scores without treating them as independent publications. Repeat the predeclared 31-row anomaly-exclusion sensitivity for each fixed arm/seed, with no refit and no changed membership elsewhere. Report stress support and numerical flags. Do **not** combine these 32 with Al-Zuhairi, Hamidi Nasab, Romano, or the three v2 folds into a manufactured publication-macro score.
5. Interpret this as one externally sourced **R=0.1 outcome-and-runout transport check** of a stress-only model under new manufacturing/finish conditions. A total improvement that worsens either the failures or runouts is a trade-off; show both. This cannot validate prospective roughness prediction or confer novel-algorithm status on synthetic self-distillation. Outcomes were already visible at intake, so describe the lock honestly as **fixed-model, prediction-before-score**, with publication overlap only bounded by the evidence above.
